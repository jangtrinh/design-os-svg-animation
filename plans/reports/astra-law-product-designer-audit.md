Status: ranked audit completed; local visual recovery implemented for owner review. The owner's “~20% similar” is feedback, not a measured parity score.

## Evidence and measurement scope

- Reference: local `/tmp/astra-for-law-reference.mp4`, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 77.594 s. Draft: `.cache/astra-for-law/proofs/frame-*.png`, captured from the deterministic player at the same timestamps. I independently extracted full-resolution reference frames with `ffmpeg -ss <seconds> -i /tmp/astra-for-law-reference.mp4 -frames:v 1`, compared aligned previews, and measured noted pixel bounds on the full-resolution frames. The local side-by-side contact strips are `/tmp/astra-law-pairs-{0,1,2}.jpg`; source timeline strips are `/tmp/astra-law-source-timeline-{0,1,2}.jpg`.
- **Fact:** `promo/astra-law/astra-law-timeline.mjs` defines 19 broad beats with uniform 0.42 s edge fades. `promo/astra-law/astra-law-scenes.mjs` gives each heading the same 28 px rise and 0.7 s ease. The reference uses typed words, camera crops, staggered tile entrances, point transformations, and variable holds. A broad beat can contain several distinct source shots.
- **Inference:** timing, staging, and missing frames explain more of the perceived gap than surface polish. This is a visual-priority judgment, not a computed similarity percentage.
- **Unknown:** the source font family, original motion curves, exact partner-tile brand asset rights, and the source project files. Use sampled pixels and verified assets, not guessed identities, as the implementation contract.

## Secondary differences after the five corrections

- **Word scene, 38–42 s:** At 39 s the two main window silhouettes are close in size and placement (both approximately x=240–1650 in the 1920 px frame). The source uses a more faithful Word toolbar, real document proportions, denser content, and a left sidebar with different message timing. Improve interior detail after the shot/camera map is stable.
- **Privacy scene, 62–64 s:** The source blurred page appears wider and shifted left versus the draft, and its blue eye glyph has a solid central element. The draft's eye outline differs. Keep the official icon rule; choose the closest licensed/source icon or document the ceiling rather than drawing an invented glyph.
- **Typography:** The source's centered titles and the draft's 118–120 px headings are often close in gross width, but the source has different weight/kerning and animates whole words and colors. Preserve source composition rather than applying a generic new design system. Exact font remains unverified.

## Fidelity ceiling and acceptance

The partner orbit and 74 s artifact collage contain third-party marks that are unavailable as verified local assets. Text-label stand-ins can match spacing and motion but cannot achieve mark-level pixel parity. The source's galaxy footage is also unavailable as a layered original; procedural geometry can match silhouette and timing, not individual pixels. Do not invent logo paths or claim a numeric parity score. Verify any replacement via SVGL or the brand owner, and keep unknown marks as honest composition placeholders.

Next review should export matched 1920 × 1080 frames at the timestamps above, compare source and draft side by side, and judge **shot identity, camera crop, word state, accent state, and silhouette** before small UI decoration. Run the existing deterministic seek and anti-flop gates after the final scene edit.
